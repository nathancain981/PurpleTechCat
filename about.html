About Nathan

👋 Hi, I'm Nathan from L25 and I run PurpleTechCat.

🐱 PurpleTechCat is my independent community IT support project, providing friendly, free tech help for local neighbours and families.

🎓 I am currently studying a BTEC Level 3 National Extended Diploma in IT and gaining hands-on experience in school, charities and IT support firm.

🏆 I have volunteered with organisations including Deaf Active, Panathlon Challenge, Switchboard and Just Like Us, helping diverse communities and developing digital skills.

🔐 I hold a clear Enhanced DBS and have experience working safely around children and SEND learners.

⚠️ PurpleTechCat is independent and is not affiliated with any school, charity, business, pub, GP surgery, cinema, or other organisation where information about the project may be displayed.

💜 My goal is simple: to make technology more accessible and less stressful for local people.
