🛡️ Cyber Safety

What is cyber safety?

Cyber safety helps protect people, devices and accounts from online threats.

How can I keep my accounts secure?

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA), and keep software up to date.

Do I need antivirus software?

Antivirus software can help detect and block malware. Keep it updated and run regular scans.

🎣 Scams & Phishing

How do I spot a phishing email?

Look for unexpected messages, suspicious links, urgent requests and spelling mistakes.

What should I do if I click a suspicious link?

Disconnect if necessary, change passwords, run security scans and monitor your accounts.

How can I report scams?

Report suspicious messages to the relevant provider and national reporting services.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Online Safety

How can I help children stay safe online?

Talk openly about online activity, use privacy settings, review age-appropriate content, encourage children to ask for help and remind them never to share personal information with strangers.

Are parental controls enough?

No. Parental controls can help reduce risks, but they work best alongside regular conversations, supervision and online safety education.

What should a child do if they feel unsafe online?

Children should stop interacting with the person or content, take screenshots if safe to do so, block and report the account where appropriate, and tell a trusted adult as soon as possible.

Where can children get help online?

Children and young people can speak to a parent, carer, teacher, safeguarding lead or another trusted adult. Specialist support is also available through Childline, NSPCC and CEOP.

What should I do if I am worried about a child online?

Take concerns seriously, listen without judgement, preserve any relevant evidence if appropriate and seek support from the relevant safeguarding professional or organisation.

What are common online risks for children?

Risks can include cyberbullying, inappropriate content, online scams, privacy breaches, grooming, unsafe communication and excessive screen time.

♿ Assistive Technology

What is assistive technology?

Assistive technology helps people access digital services through features such as captions, screen readers and magnification.

What accessibility features are available on smartphones?

Many devices include captions, text-to-speech, speech-to-text, magnification and hearing accessibility tools.

🚨 Reporting Cybercrime

How do I report cybercrime in the UK?

Fraud, scams and cybercrime to Report Fraud (England, Wales and Northern Ireland).

Phishing emails to the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) by forwarding them to report@phishing.gov.uk.

Scam text messages by forwarding them to 7726.

Child online exploitation or grooming concerns to CEOP.

What information should I keep as evidence?

Keep screenshots, emails, messages, dates and any relevant account information.
