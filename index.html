Welcome

PurpleTechCat is an independent community IT support project, providing free tech help for local families and neighbours across the L25 area.

PurpleTechCat provide friendly, patient and accessible support with everyday digital problems, devices, connectivity, accessibility tools and online safety.

☀️ Weekends: 9am–10:30am

What PurpleTechCat can do?

  • 💻 Slow computers — making your laptop or PC run better
  • 📱 Phones & tablets — setting up apps, accounts, or new devices
  • 🌐 Wi‑Fi problems — fixing home internet issues and checking router settings
  • 🔢 IP address checks — helping you understand devices on your home network
  • 🛜 Home network checks — identifying your default gateway and helping you safely access your router’s browser interface to view Wi‑Fi settings and connected devices
  • 🖨️ Printer issues — when it won’t print or keeps showing errors
  • 🔐 Passwords & accounts — help logging in or resetting details
  • 🎮 Gaming setup — consoles, updates, or connection problems
  • 🛠️ Installing software — adding programs safely
  • 🧩 Making devices easier to use — accessibility, simple layouts, clear settings

What PurpleTechCat can’t do?

  • 🔧 Broken screens or hardware repairs — anything needing tools
  • ⚡ Electrical work — plugs, wiring, or dangerous equipment
  • 🏢 Business IT systems — only community and home support
  • 🔒 Sensitive personal data — banking, medical, or private documents
  • 🚨 Emergency or urgent support — not a 24/7 service
  • 💼 Paid work — PurpleTechCat is free and community‑based
  • ⚠️ Risky or unsafe tasks — anything that could cause harm
  • 🛡️ Advanced networking — no enterprise routers, firewalls or server configuration
